Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Be Studios respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we use information in connection with our customer communication and studio support services.

Information we may receive

When you contact Be Studios through services such as WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook, we may receive information made available through those platforms, including:

  • Your name and contact details;
  • Messages and other information you choose to send us;
  • Information needed to respond to questions about classes, bookings, schedules, memberships and studio services.

How we use this information

We use this information only to provide customer service and operate Be Studios. This may include reviewing customer messages, preparing and sending replies, helping with class and booking enquiries, and assisting customers with memberships and other studio services.

Use of Meta Platform Data

Information received through Meta products is used only to provide and improve the customer communication service described above. We do not sell Meta Platform Data or use it for unrelated purposes.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate our communication, booking or business systems, only where needed to provide the service and subject to appropriate safeguards.

Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide customer service, manage studio operations, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of the use of your personal information. You may also object to certain processing or request a copy of your data.

Data deletion requests

If you would like us to delete personal information associated with your interactions with Be Studios, please contact us using the email address below and include enough information for us to identify your request.
